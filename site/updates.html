Atlas findings with 2026 studies

  • Replicated On average, people with ME/CFS have mildly lower free cortisol (salivary awakening and morning cortisol, 24-h urinary cortisol and hair cortisol) than healthy controls.Hormones
  • Several studies Challenge tests show a blunted cortisol response to ACTH and exaggerated cortisol suppression after a glucocorticoid dose, consistent with enhanced negative feedback.Hormones
  • Contested Lower cortisol in long COVID, reported in a 2023 Nature cohort, has not held up in later clinic and cohort studies that controlled for sampling time.Hormones
  • Early Women with CFS report more endometriosis, heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, hysterectomy and earlier menopause than controls in US population-based case-control studies.Hormones
  • Early ME/CFS genetic risk is enriched in brain tissues and the pituitary, but genetically predicted morning cortisol does not change risk.Hormones
  • Replicated The genome-wide DecodeME signal replicates in independent cohorts: genetic correlation 0.65-0.73 with the Million Veteran Program CFS GWAS and 0.80-0.90 with UK Biobank CFS, plus concordant directions for sub-threshold SNPs in FinnGen.Genetics
  • Several studies Genome-wide ME/CFS heritability is enriched in brain tissues and neurons (including glutamatergic synapse genes and subcortical/cerebellar neuron types), with no enrichment in blood immune cells.Genetics
  • Early A 2026 UK Biobank GWAS with stricter, multi-source case definitions (1,268 cases) reports 7 loci that replicate in disjoint UK Biobank or All of Us cohorts (near BICD1, GRIN2A, CSMD1, RORA, CLYBL), though none replicate in all three.Genetics
  • Early MR of DecodeME suggests higher mitochondrial DNA copy number is protective and higher GlycA and mean platelet volume raise risk, while genetically predicted metabolite leads (IPA, kynurenine, ketones, carnitine, bile acids) and vitamin D, BMI and blood pressure are null or unreplicated.Genetics
  • Contested Proprietary combinatorial SNP analyses (PrecisionLife) report thousands of multi-SNP signatures in UK Biobank and DecodeME, with top vs bottom decile of signature count giving OR 1.64.Genetics
  • Contested TRPM3 ion channel dysfunction in NK cells and TRPM3 SNPs are reported repeatedly by one research group, but the SNP studies are tiny and no TRPM3 association appears in GWAS.Genetics
  • Several studies Joint hypermobility and Ehlers-Danlos syndrome are over-represented in ME/CFS (Beighton ≥4 in 60% of children with CFS vs 24% of controls; 50% in a Swedish severe cohort; EDS about 100x expected in Finnish registries).Genetics
  • Early The replicated deficit of aromatic lactates (indolelactate, phenyllactate, 4-hydroxyphenyllactate) in ME/CFS blood is not predicted by stool bacteria or bacterial genes, so it is probably host-side or due to absorption, not dysbiosis.Gut and microbiome
  • Replicated IBS is about seven times more common in ME/CFS (pooled OR 7.20; prevalence about 37%), and IBS status drives much of the measured microbiome difference.Gut and microbiome
  • Early ME/CFS genetic risk overlaps with IBS and is enriched in enteric neurons, but genetically predicted gut-derived metabolites (IPA, indolelactate, kynurenine, bile acids) do not change ME/CFS risk.Gut and microbiome
  • Early Reduced TRPM3 ion-channel current and calcium influx in patients' NK cells has been reported repeatedly, including a two-site study (36 vs 42), but only ever by one research group.Immune system
  • Refuted Elevated IgG against beta2-adrenergic and M3/M4 muscarinic receptors, reported in about 30% of patients by ELISA, is not reproduced by unbiased high-throughput antigen profiling.Immune system
  • Refuted Removing autoantibodies by immunoadsorption helps in open-label cohorts but fails against sham control, and antibody removal does not predict who improves.Immune system
  • Several studies IgG from long COVID patients transfers pain, fatigue-like behaviour and small-fibre nerve damage to mice in three independent 2026 studies; no equivalent published ME/CFS transfer experiment exists.Immune system
  • Early Purified IgG from ME/CFS patients fragments mitochondria and shifts energetics in human endothelial cells, an effect absent with multiple-sclerosis and healthy IgG and lost on Fab/Fc cleavage.Immune system
  • Several studies Long COVID immune profiling finds exhausted T cells, myeloid shifts, raised antibodies to non-SARS-CoV-2 viruses especially EBV, and low cortisol, giving a reference phenotype for post-infectious illness.Immune system
  • Early Newly reported elevations of serum IL-11 and MMP-9 in female ME/CFS patients are unreplicated single-cohort findings.Immune system
  • Early Plasma galectin-9 and artemin are elevated in long COVID and proposed as biomarkers, with links to cognitive symptoms; data mostly from one group.Immune system
  • Replicated After infectious mononucleosis in adolescents, 13%, 7% and 4% met paediatric CFS criteria at 6, 12 and 24 months; all 13 cases still ill at 24 months were female.Infection and viruses
  • Replicated In RECOVER-Adult, new ME/CFS (IOM criteria) developed at 2.66 per 100 person-years after SARS-CoV-2 infection vs 0.93 in uninfected participants (HR 4.93), and 4.5% of infected participants met criteria.Infection and viruses
  • Early Charité studies report a deficient EBV-specific memory B- and T-cell response in CFS (absent or low EBNA-1/VCA antibody-secreting memory B cells in up to 76% of patients, fewer polyfunctional EBNA-1-specific T cells), while CMV responses were normal.Infection and viruses
  • Early Genetic instruments for higher EBV DNA load in blood (Kamitaki 2026) are associated with ME/CFS risk in the Million Veteran Program (OR 1.59, p=0.012) but not significantly in DecodeME (OR 1.13, p=0.25).Infection and viruses
  • Early Antibodies against herpesvirus dUTPases (EBV, HHV-6, VZV), proposed as a marker of lytic or abortive-lytic reactivation, are higher in ME/CFS in studies from one lab, including a 2026 longitudinal study (873 serum samples, 40 patients vs 16 controls) where levels tracked fatigue severity.Infection and viruses
  • Contested Enteroviral VP1 protein was found in stomach biopsies from 82% of 165 CFS patients vs 20% of controls, with EV RNA in 37% vs 5%, but this has not been independently replicated with blinded methods.Infection and viruses
  • Several studies SARS-CoV-2 spike, S1 or nucleocapsid antigen is detectable in plasma more often after COVID than in pre-pandemic samples (excess +11% at 3-6 months, +5.4% at 10-14 months), with more antigenemia after more severe acute illness.Infection and viruses
  • Refuted Three randomised placebo-controlled trials of 15-25 days of nirmatrelvir-ritonavir in established long COVID (STOP-PASC n=155, PAX LC n=100, RECOVER-VITAL n=959; about 1,214 people in total) found no benefit on primary or secondary outcomes.Infection and viruses
  • Early Treating acute COVID-19 with nirmatrelvir may reduce later long COVID: a small terminated Norwegian RCT found 26% vs 43% at 3 months (RR 0.60), echoing observational VA data, but larger prospective evidence is lacking.Infection and viruses
  • Refuted Temelimab, an antibody against HERV-W envelope protein, did not reduce fatigue versus placebo in a 203-person RCT of HERV-W-positive post-COVID patients.Infection and viruses
  • Early Unbiased blood virome/EV sequencing does not find more viruses in ME/CFS; a TTMV subgroup signal is unreplicated.Infection and viruses
  • Replicated On a single maximal exercise test, ME/CFS patients have lower peak oxygen uptake (pooled about 5 ml/kg/min below controls) with a low peak heart rate (chronotropic incompetence).Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Contested ME/CFS patients perform worse on a second maximal exercise test 24 h after the first, most clearly in workload and VO2 at the ventilatory threshold, but two independent groups (Lien 2019; Mancini and Natelson 2026) did not reproduce the VO2 effect.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Early ME/CFS muscle has a lower proportion of type I (oxidative) fibres, type I-specific atrophy, lower mitochondrial capacity and fewer capillaries per fibre, a pattern that 60 days of strict bed rest in healthy people did not reproduce.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Refuted The claim that ME/CFS serum changes healthy myoblast mitochondrial respiration failed a pre-registered replication of 67 patients vs 53 controls, though a small 3D engineered-muscle study reported weaker contraction with patient serum.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Several studies Repeated maximal handgrip testing shows lower strength, faster decline across repetitions and poorer recovery after 60 minutes in ME/CFS, correlating with severity and PEM.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Several studies Several designs (aerobic-capacity-matched controls, idiopathic-fatigue and MS comparison groups, and a 60-day bed-rest model) indicate that deconditioning alone does not explain the exercise and muscle abnormalities in ME/CFS.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Several studies People with long COVID have lower peak VO2 than recovered controls on average (about 5 ml/kg/min), with patterns pointing to deconditioning, chronotropic incompetence and impaired peripheral extraction rather than a cardiac or ventilatory limit.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Several studies Invasive CPET in long COVID with exercise intolerance repeatedly shows low systemic oxygen extraction with normal or high cardiac output, and in a subset reduced biventricular filling (preload failure), mirroring ME/CFS iCPET findings.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Several studies Muscle oxidative capacity measured by 31P-MRS (slower PCr recovery) or NIRS is reduced in long COVID, though the deficit does not consistently correlate with fatigue.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Contested Whether standardised exercise provokes measurable post-exertional symptom worsening in long COVID is contested: a crossover RCT found no greater 48-h fatigue than controls, while symptom-tracking studies find variable PESE peaking within 72 h in subsets.Muscle, exertion and energy
  • Replicated Blood flow to the brain falls much more than normal during head-up tilt in ME/CFS (about 26% vs 7% in controls), including in patients whose heart rate and blood pressure stay normal.Nervous system and brain
  • Replicated Many patients over-breathe when upright and lower their end-tidal CO2. This drives part of the fall in brain blood flow, and correcting it with phenylephrine restored blood flow and working memory in one small trial.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested POTS occurs in a minority of ME/CFS patients, and published rates vary widely (about 6-43%) with the setting, the age group and the test protocol.Nervous system and brain
  • Several studies On the 10-minute NASA lean test, a subgroup of recent-onset patients shows a rising heart rate with an abnormally narrow pulse pressure. Autonomic specialists say the test has not been validated against tilt or active stand.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested Skin biopsies show reduced small-nerve-fibre density in about 31-53% of ME/CFS patients from specialist referral cohorts, but the controlled NIH study found no difference from healthy volunteers.Nervous system and brain
  • Several studies Long COVID patients referred for neuropathy evaluation often have small-fibre neuropathy on skin biopsy, including loss of autonomic fibres to sweat glands and hair muscles.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested Conventional structural MRI (grey matter volume, routine clinical reads) shows no consistent abnormality in ME/CFS. Reported regional volume differences rarely replicate across groups.Nervous system and brain
  • Early Advanced diffusion MRI models report widespread white-matter differences in ME/CFS that conventional DTI misses, and the pattern may differ between post-infectious and gradual-onset cases.Nervous system and brain
  • Several studies Brainstem differences (volume larger or smaller, altered signal or connectivity) are among the more often repeated imaging findings in ME/CFS, though the methods differ between studies.Nervous system and brain
  • Early During a sustained grip task, ME/CFS patients fatigue earlier while their brain motor-network activity fails to ramp up as it does in controls (NIH; reduced temporoparietal-junction activation).Nervous system and brain
  • Several studies Brain lactate measured by MR spectroscopy is raised in ME/CFS: ventricular CSF lactate in three cohorts from one group, and thalamic or regional lactate in two other groups. It is also raised in depression, so it is not specific.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested In long COVID, one [11C]PBR28 PET study found widespread raised TSPO signal that correlated with vascular markers, and a [11C]PK11195 study found no group difference.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested Resting (supine) cerebral blood flow by ASL or SPECT is reported as lower in some ME/CFS studies, but the results are inconsistent and often confounded by unmeasured orthostatic intolerance.Nervous system and brain
  • Early In the NIH cohort, CSF levels of the catechol metabolites DOPA, DOPAC and DHPG were lower in post-infectious ME/CFS. Noradrenaline and dopamine did not differ.Nervous system and brain
  • Contested CSF cytokine and proteome studies report differences (e.g. raised eotaxin/CCL11, distinct immunotypes), but no CSF protein signature has replicated, and ME/CFS and fibromyalgia CSF proteomes were indistinguishable.Nervous system and brain
  • Early Uncontrolled clinic series report high rates of craniocervical 'obstruction', low-lying tonsils and signs of raised intracranial pressure in severe ME/CFS, and surgical case reports describe improvement. There are no controlled imaging studies or trials.Nervous system and brain
