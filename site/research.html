About a quarter of patients are housebound or bedbound, yet 7 of 556 active projects say in their title that they study severe patients. Crashes after exertion define the illness, and 22 name exertion or PEM. The open questions page sets out which experiments would most change what we know.

Trials in progress

152 treatment trials are running or about to start: 25 in ME/CFS, 16 in both, and 111 in long COVID. The most active countries are USA (49), Germany (16), Canada (14), Spain (10), China (6). Each trial shows its sites, sponsor, investigators and expected end date, all taken from its registry entry on 2026-11-08. Within each group, ME/CFS trials come first, then the largest. The trials page covers results from trials that have finished.
