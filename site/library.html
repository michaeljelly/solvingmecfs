Results that contradict an atlas finding 1
  • The effectiveness of activity pacing interventions for people with chronic fatigue syndrome: a systematic review and meta-analysis.Casson S et al. · 2023Meta-analysis of 14 RCTs: activity pacing interventions reduced fatigue (Hedges g -0.52, 95% CI -0.73 to -0.32), psychological distress (-0.37) and depression (-0.29) and improved physical function (MD 7.18) versus no treatment/usual care, with larger effects for interventions that encouraged graded escalation of activity. Self-management: pacing

  • Persistent symptoms and clinical findings in adults with post-acute sequelae of COVID-19/post-COVID-19 syndrome in the second year after acute infection: A population-based, nested case-control study.Peter RS et al. · 2025In a German population-based nested case-control study (982 PCS, 576 controls), 67.6% of PCS cases persisted into year two; persistent PCS had lower VO2max (27.9 vs 31.0 ml/kg/min), handgrip (40.2 vs 42.5 kg) and higher VE/VCO2 slope than recovered controls, with no lab, cardiac, EBV or viral-persistence differences; 35.6% reported PEM >14 h and 11.6% ME/CFS-compatible symptoms. Exercise capacity (single CPET) PEM definition and timing

Post-infectious onset

  • Post-COVID-19 Condition Fatigue Outcomes Among Danish Residents.O'Regan E et al. · 2024 · 50115In 50,115 Danish residents (25,249 test-positive vs 24,866 test-negative, mostly vaccinated), infection was linked to a 3% higher fatigue score (SR 1.03) and higher odds of PEM (OR 2.04, 1.81-2.30) 2-18 months later; hospitalisation raised fatigue scores 23%; prior psychiatric conditions did not modify risk. Post-infectious onset PEM definition and timing
  • Adrenergic dysfunction in patients with myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome and fibromyalgia: A systematic review and meta-analysis.Hendrix J et al. · 2025 · 543 ME/CFS, 747 controlsMeta-analysis of 37 studies (543 ME/CFS, 651 FM vs 747 and 447 controls) found elevated baseline adrenaline in ME/CFS (SMD 0.49, 95% CI 0.31-0.67) and atypical adrenergic responses to exercise, with adrenergic dysfunction mainly in ME/CFS rather than fibromyalgia. Autonomic
  • Cardiopulmonary, metabolic, and perceptual responses during exercise in Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS): A Multi-site Clinical Assessment of ME/CFS (MCAM) sub-study.Cook DB et al. · 2022 · 214 vs 189; matched 99 vs 99In 214 ME/CFS vs 189 controls across six clinics (90% met peak-effort criteria), ME/CFS had lower ventilation, breathing frequency, HR and ventilatory efficiency and more chronotropic incompetence across 20-100% peak VO2, with higher ventilatory equivalents and RPE, including in 99 pairs matched for peak VO2.   • Evidence of Clinical Pathology Abnormalities in People with Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS) from an Analytic Cross-Sectional Study.Nacul L et al. · 2019 · 272 vs 136In UK ME/CFS Biobank labs (272 ME/CFS, 136 controls), serum CK was lower in severe ME/CFS (median 54 U/L) than in controls (101.5) and non-severe cases (84), and this held after adjusting for sex, age, BMI, muscle mass, duration and activity (OR 0.05 vs controls). Muscle proteins in blood
  • Postural orthostatic tachycardia is not a useful diagnostic marker for chronic fatigue syndrome.Roerink ME et al. · 2017 · 419 CFS vs 341 non-CFS fatiguedOn active stand, POTS was present in 5.7% of 419 adults with CFS vs 6.9% of non-CFS fatigued adults (P=0.54), and 18.2% vs 17.4% in adolescents; POTS was unrelated to severity or activity.
