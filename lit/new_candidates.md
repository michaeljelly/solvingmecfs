# Candidate new findings (relation=new, importance >= 2)

## immune (2)
- i2 Steiner 2020 [ME/CFS] (305 vs 201, genetic): PTPN22 rs2476601 (OR 1.63) and CTLA4 rs3087243 (OR 1.53) autoimmunity alleles associated with ME/CFS only in infection-triggered onset. https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2020.00578
- i2 Petty 2016 [ME/CFS] (?, case-control): 34 miRNAs raised in PBMC; four validated (miR-99b, miR-330, miR-126, miR-30c), strongest in NK cells in an independent cohort. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150904

## infection (1)
- i3 Reis 2026 [long COVID] (399, RCT): Fluvoxamine reduced fatigue (FSS difference -0.43 at day 60, -0.58 at day 90) vs placebo; metformin inconclusive and stopped for futility in one arm. https://doi.org/10.7326/annals-26-02144

## muscle-exertion (11)
- i3 Hendrix 2025 [ME/CFS] (543 ME/CFS, 747 controls, meta-analysis): Meta-analysis of 37 studies: adrenaline raised in ME/CFS (SMD 0.49, 95% CI 0.31-0.67) with atypical exercise responses; adrenergic dysfunction mainly in ME/CFS rather than fibromyalgia. https://doi.org/10.1111/eci.14318
- i2 Matits 2026 [long COVID] (228 (128 vs 100), case-control): Long COVID had lower relative circulating cell-free mtDNA and higher CRP (25% low-grade inflammation); cognition correlated with markers. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-026-03471-0
- i2 Souma 2026 [ME/CFS] (92 vs 44, case-control): Lower baseline irisin in ME and elevated TSP-1 linked to severity; TSP-1 inhibits irisin signalling in vitro; samples before and after 90-min mechanical stress. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27114770
- i2 Weber-Fahr 2026 [both] (27 vs 23, case-control): Whole-brain 31P-MRSI shows reduced ATP/PCr ratios in cingulate cortex in post-COVID condition; ACC value correlates with cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2026.01.004
- i2 An 2024 [long COVID] (?, meta-analysis): Pooled PEM prevalence in post-acute COVID syndrome at >=3 months was 55% (95% CI 38-71%); DSQ-PEM most used tool. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.genhosppsych.2024.10.011
- i2 Bermejo-Guerrero 2023 [ME/CFS] (?, case-control): Serum GDF-15 distinguishes primary mitochondrial myopathy from CFS https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12062435
- i2 Jammes 2020 [ME/CFS] (72 (39 vs 33), cohort): Resting and exercising muscle membrane excitability (M-wave) altered in 39 patients who also had higher oxidative stress markers and lower exercise K+ outflow; 33 patients had none, indicating subgroups. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-020-02341-9
- i2 Melvin 2019 [ME/CFS] (150 ME/CFS vs 150 controls vs 50 MS, case-control): Serum GDF15 higher in severe ME/CFS (602 vs 491 pg/ml in controls, P=0.01) but only marginally in mild/moderate; correlated with fatigue; stable over 7 months. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-019-02153-6
- i2 Carpallo-Porcar 2026, preprint [long COVID] (?, RCT): SPM-enriched supplement plus telerehabilitation vs control for fatigue, dyspnea and QoL in PCC (ARACOV-02). https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9354365/v1
- i2 Germann 2026, preprint [long COVID] (114 (24 per-protocol analysis), RCT): 16-week home taVNS: VAS fatigue improved 11.9 points after 8 weeks of active but not sham or placebo in adherent participants (p=0.003, N=24). https://doi.org/10.64898/2025.12.30.25343085
- i2 Goodship 2025, preprint [both] (?, cohort): App data: symptom severity varied across the menstrual cycle (lowest in early luteal phase), crashes more frequent during menstruation, and combined hormonal contraception users reported lower symptoms (OR 0.83) and fewer crashes (OR 0.55; n=70). https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.01.24.25321092

## neuro (5)
- i2 Mischke 2026 [long COVID] (71, case-control): Impaired auditory sensory gating (P50 suppression) as a neurophysiological correlate of cognitive fatigue in long COVID. https://doi.org/10.1037/neu0001120
- i2 Wirth 2026 [ME/CFS] (6077 vs 5x, case-control): ICD-10 codes in year before ME/CFS diagnosis in children and young people suggest early indicators (1:5 matched claims data). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40848-1
- i2 Fujimoto 2025 [long COVID] (30 vs 80, imaging): [11C]K-2 PET shows systemically increased AMPA receptor density in Cog-LC versus healthy controls, with cytokine associations. https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcaf337
- i2 Fletcher 2010 [ME/CFS] (93 vs 100, case-control): Plasma neuropeptide Y was elevated in CFS compared with controls and GWI and correlated with symptom severity. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-76
- i2 Kerr 2010 [ME/CFS] (200 vs 200, case-control): Parvovirus B19 seroprevalence similar (75% vs 78%) but anti-NS1 IgG found in 41.5% of CFS vs 7% of donors (OR 9.42), and B19 DNA in 11 CFS vs 0 controls. https://doi.org/10.1099/vir.0.017590-0

## omics (2)
- i2 Nepotchatykh 2023 [ME/CFS] (?, case-control): Circulating microRNA signatures discriminate ME/CFS from fibromyalgia and comorbid conditions https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-28955-9
- i2 Maloney 2010 [ME/CFS] (111 CFS, 259 ISF, 123 controls, case-control): CFS had 2.12-fold odds of metabolic syndrome (OR 2.12, 1.06-4.23) with a graded relationship. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.metabol.2009.12.019

## vascular (11)
- i2 Wu 2026 [long COVID] (108, case-control): Anterior cerebral arteriopathy score higher in post-COVID brain fog and headache (combined 6.6 vs control 0.8), suggesting cerebral hypoperfusion in brain fog. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00406-026-02276-0
- i2 Sommen 2026 [long COVID] (?, case-control): CyTOF immunophenotyping of young females 6 months after mild COVID found an immune signature for fatigue across long COVID and fatigued controls via machine learning. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-026-08149-3
- i2 Tamariz 2026 [long COVID] (185, cross-sectional): Higher whole blood viscosity associated with higher COMPASS-31 (B=7.0, p=0.01) but not overall symptom burden; POTS/OH 23% in tertile 1 vs 32% in tertile 3. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ahjo.2026.100776
- i2 Visconti 2025 [long COVID] (?, cohort): Fatigued long COVID patients had higher myostatin and irisin, with no difference in lung function, ultrasound or LV systolic function; ICU admission raised fatigue risk (OR 2.65). https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1547886
- i2 Wang 2025 [long COVID] (about 70, RCT): Low-level tragus stimulation lowered orthostatic heart-rate rise, NPY and shifted HRV (higher HF, lower LF/HF) over 1 month in post-COVID POTS. https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.70110
- i2 Baraniuk 2017 [ME/CFS] (CFS 43 nonexercise; GWI 22, controls 22 (post-exercise groups 15-42), case-control): CSF miRNAs were similar at rest across groups; after exercise CFS had 12 diminished miRNAs, with patterns distinct from Gulf War Illness. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-15383-9
- i2 Lidbury 2017 [ME/CFS] (?, cross-sectional): Serum activin B was elevated in CFS/ME vs controls and reference interval; activin A, follistatin and routine pathology were normal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-017-1161-4
- i2 Rowe 2016 [ME/CFS] (60 CFS vs 20 HC, RCT): Passive straight leg raise (neuromuscular strain) vs sham increased pain, concentration difficulty, composite symptoms during and 24 h after in CFS (composite P=0.005 at 24 h). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0159386
- i2 Maes 2009 [ME/CFS] (58 vs 22, case-control): Plasma CoQ10 significantly lower in ME/CFS (p=0.00001); 44.8% below the lowest control value; inversely related to fatigue and autonomic symptom scores. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20010505/
- i2 Streeten DH. 2001 [ME/CFS] (7 vs 7, case-control): CFS patients had delayed orthostatic hypotension and reduced foot-vein contractile compliance to norepinephrine, implying impaired venous sympathetic innervation and leg venous pooling. https://doi.org/10.1097/00000441-200103000-00001
- i2 Abd-Eldayem 2026, preprint [long COVID] (?, case-control): LC-POTS monocytes have increased mitochondrial content, superoxide and isolevuglandins, T cell-monocyte doublets with IFN-g/IL-17A; tVNS reduced doublets and IL-6 in a subset. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.08.26352776
