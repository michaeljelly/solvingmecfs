# Data catalogue: licence audit

Date: 27 October 2026. Catalogue: `site/data/catalogue.json` (110 entries). Bucket: R2 `mecfs-data`, public at https://data.solvingmecfs.org/ (fallback https://pub-befaaf5d33ee4324b51dc65bd8a75aa2.r2.dev/).

## Summary

- 43 ids rehosted (33 third-party datasets or repositories, 10 of our analysis bundles); 67 ids link only.
- Uploaded 3270 objects, 3.50 GB (including READMEs and LICENSES.md). 1 large plain-text table (>20 MB) was gzipped before upload. Failed uploads: none remaining (one file with brackets in its name, `EEfRT/gee[50].pdf`, failed under curl URL globbing and was uploaded from a renamed copy as `gee_50_.pdf`).
- Rule used: rehost only when the source states a licence that allows redistribution (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain, NCBI GEO open data, MIT/BSD/Apache/GPL for repositories). Anything with no licence, NonCommercial/NoDerivatives terms, login, data-use agreement or ambiguous terms is link only.
- Licences were checked with curl against each source's API or page (OSF API, Crossref, Europe PMC, Zenodo API, figshare API, PRIDE API, MetaboLights API, Metabolomics Workbench REST, raw.githubusercontent.com LICENSE files, EBI/GWAS Catalog pages, NCBI FTP banner). Pages that could not be read by curl are noted.

## Problems and caveats

- NCBI GEO disclaimer, ClinicalTrials.gov terms, FinnGen and Pan-UKB documentation pages could not be read by curl (browser checks or 404). GEO was classed REHOST on NCBI's published open-data policy and the FTP banner; FinnGen and ClinicalTrials.gov raw data were kept link only.
- Metabolomics Workbench now reports CC BY-NC-ND for every ME/CFS study checked (19 studies), so all MW data are link only, including ST003178 (the NIH CSF values are also in the CC BY Nature Communications Source Data, which is rehosted).
- Germain 2022 xlsx was taken from the BioMapAI repository copy; it is the article's supplementary dataset (JCI Insight, CC BY 4.0). Guo 2023 tables come from the PMC author-manuscript supplement; the version of record is CC BY 4.0.
- The GSE214283 article (Cell Rep Med) is CC BY-NC-ND but the GEO deposit is under NCBI policy; only GEO-derived files are mirrored.
- GPL-3.0 repositories (NIH Walitt code, UAB cohort, thesis) are mirrored with their LICENSE files; GPL is a free licence that permits redistribution with the licence attached.
- Several datasets have open licences but were not kept locally (DecodeME+MVP meta, Zenodo Olink sets, methylation GEO sets, ENA/SRA repertoire reads, Rydland, Bragée, UK Biobank Beentjes supplement, Ryback 2026). They are link only in the catalogue; they could be mirrored later.
- Custom domain data.solvingmecfs.org answered HTTP 200 during upload; r2.dev fallback also verified.
