# Funding recommendations (as of 29 Oct 2026)

Full records and verification flags are in `calls.json`.

## Apply now

1. **Anthropic guest investigators**: closes about **5 Nov 2026** (estimated). In-kind support: 8–12 weeks with Anthropic researchers. PIs at academic or non-profit labs only; public data only. Suits data re-analysis or an open PEM-tracking tool.
2. **Solve M.E. Ramsay Scholars**: 800-word plan due **13 Nov 2026**. $50k–150k for early-career researchers, newcomers to ME/CFS and collaborations. No country limit. Suits pilots of crash-window, serum-factor or pituitary work.
3. **MRC Proof of Concept, stage one**: closes **18 Nov 2026**. Suits turning a serum factor into a diagnostic test, or preparing a trial of a repurposed drug. Snippets only.
4. **MRC grants under the ME/CFS highlight notice**: **rolling**, with decisions in June and December. MRC's first priority is the mechanism of PEM, which crash-window sampling plus circulatory phenotyping addresses. This is the main UK route.
5. **Wellcome Discovery Award**: **rolling**. From today the lead must be based in the UK (or certain low- and middle-income countries). Average award £3.5M, for established PIs.
6. **NIH parent R21 / R01**: **16 Feb / 5 Feb 2027**. Non-US applicants can apply, but foreign subawards are not funded. The R21 ($275k over 2 years) suits feasibility pilots.

## Prepare for next rounds

- **ME Research UK** runs one call a year; the next outline deadline is likely around January 2027.
- **Long COVID Research Fund** usually takes letters of intent in the autumn and is open worldwide. Best charity fit for autonomic and circulatory work; 2026 dates unconfirmed.
- **MRC Experimental Medicine** (the 2026 round closed 7 Oct).
- **NIHR i4i, RfPB and EME** run recurring rounds; dates unverified.
- **Horizon Europe 2027 post-infection topic**: UK groups are eligible.
- **NINDS ME/CFS P20**: forecast but unpublished; would need a US lead.

## Experiment fit

- **Crash-window sampling:** Ramsay, MRC, NIH R21, MRC Experimental Medicine
- **Circulatory phenotyping:** MRC, Long COVID Research Fund, Ramsay
- **Serum-factor tests:** Ramsay, MRC Proof of Concept, Anthropic. Biobanks: Simmaron, AusME
- **Pituitary testing:** Ramsay, MRC, NIH R21
- **Patient-tracking software:** Anthropic, NIHR i4i/RfPB, Innovate UK, NIH SBIR (US companies only)
- **Trials:** MRC Proof of Concept, NIHR EME, RECOVER-TLC idea portal (not funding)
- **Data re-analysis:** Anthropic, Ramsay, MRC, Wellcome

## Gaps

- No open ME/CFS-specific government call in the UK, US, Canada, Norway or the Netherlands.
- No dedicated funding for pituitary or endocrine work, or for sampling severely affected patients at home.
- Little for patient-tracking software, and almost nothing for non-academic developers.
- ME/CFS trial calls are closed or need a national lead: NIHR's platform-trial award (closed), BMFTR (Germany), MRFF (Australia).
- OMF, PolyBio and Simmaron fund by invitation only.
- No prize funders found. No "Amar Foundation" found.

Blocked sites, marked unverified: OMF, ME Association, NIHR, NHMRC, ZonMw.
