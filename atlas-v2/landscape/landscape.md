## Absorb so that we become a superset

1. **Research projects and people** (MRR model): projects, labs, networks and funders, linked to findings. Seed this from MRR under agreement, then extend it beyond the six countries.
2. **Live trial status**: pull ClinicalTrials.gov, DRKS, CTIS and ICTRP the way jbkze and Long Covid Studies do, and attach the trials to treatments and hypotheses.
3. **LC RCT library**: import the 185 RCTs from Bond/L.OVE and the EPPI quality scores into the treatments page, then keep that library current, since both look paused.
4. **Patient-reported outcomes**: show TREATME ratings next to each treatment verdict.
5. **A dated timeline per topic** (hanneseh) and an **Atom feed and JSON export** of changes.
6. **Machine-readable claims**: give findings HPO/GO/MONDO IDs and verbatim quotes, compatible with dismech.
7. **CDE v2 mapping** for each dataset in the catalogue, and the **mapMECFS metadata** (119 datasets), which is at risk.
8. **Context content** we lack: history, people, orgs and audience primers (MEpedia), and a German interface (MRR).
9. **Visible critique**: link each finding to its S4ME thread and ME/CFS Science post.

## Link to or partner on instead of duplicating

- **MRR** for the European project register: link and exchange data, don't rebuild it.
- **Long Covid Studies, Solve M.E. and PLRC Registry** for trial recruitment.
- **mapMECFS, RECOVER BDC, DecodeME** for controlled-access data: link out, rehosting only what licences allow.
- **S4ME** for discussion, rather than hosting our own forum.
- **Health Rising, ME/CFS Science, newsletters** for news and commentary.
- **NINDS CDEs** as the measurement standard.
- **EUROMENE**: cite as an archive only.
